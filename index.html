The Founder Story

“Ever since I was a little girl, fashion has always fascinated me. From styling my sisters and myself by draping some bed sheets, wearing my mom's heels and walking down an imaginary ramp for a fashion week where every time I walked as a designer instilled the sheer joy, beauty, and love for fashion. And today, as I live this passion every day, it feels like more than just a dream come true.

Fashion is never just a career choice for me; it is something I feel deeply within. I am constantly drawn to the beauty of fabrics, the emotion behind silhouettes, and the stories that clothing can tell. Being a fashion designer and stylist is not just about creating—it is a journey of passion, patience, and believing in my vision.

House of Kamakhya is deeply personal to me. It is not just a brand, but a piece of my soul where tradition, culture, and modern expression come together. Every design I create carries a story, a feeling, and a part of who I am. Through my work, I want people to not just wear my designs, but truly feel them.”

Founder Rakshitha MH

Diploma in Fashion Apparel Designing
BSc in Fashion and Apparel Designing

Each motif is a small reminder of home, patience, and quiet strength.

Our Vision

To create pieces that let people express who they are today while carrying the beauty of yesterday. Traditional artistry, modern tailoring, and effortless wearability for daily wear, festive moments, and statement outfits.
